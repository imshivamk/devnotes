
### 1. Rectangular Cartesian Co-ordinates & Points

The foundation of coordinate geometry involves the $X$ and $Y$ axes and the distance between points.

#### **Core Concepts & Formulas**

- **Distance Formula**: The distance between $P(x_1, y_1)$ and $Q(x_2, y_2)$ is $d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}$.
    
- **Section Formula**:
    
    - **Internal Division**: $x = \frac{mx_2+nx_1}{m+n}, y = \frac{my_2+ny_1}{m+n}$.
        
    - **External Division**: $x = \frac{mx_2-nx_1}{m-n}, y = \frac{my_2-ny_1}{m-n}$.
        
- **Midpoint Formula**: $(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1+y_2}{2})$.
    
- **Centroid of a Triangle**: $G(\frac{x_1+x_2+x_3}{3}, \frac{y_1+y_2+y_3}{3})$.
    

#### **Special Cases**

- **Area of Triangle**: $\frac{1}{2} |x_1(y_2-y_3) + x_2(y_3-y_1) + x_3(y_1-y_2)|$.
    
- **Collinearity**: Three points are collinear if the area of the triangle formed by them is $0$.
    

> **Solved Example**: Find the distance between $(3, -4)$ and $(-2, 8)$.
> 
> **Step 1**: $x_1=3, y_1=-4, x_2=-2, y_2=8$.
> 
> **Step 2**: $d = \sqrt{(-2-3)^2 + (8 - (-4))^2} = \sqrt{(-5)^2 + (12)^2}$.
> 
> **Solution**: $\sqrt{25 + 144} = \sqrt{169} = 13$ units.

### 2. Straight Lines & Pairs of Lines

This topic covers the various forms of line equations and the relationship between multiple lines.

#### **Core Concepts & Formulas**

- **Slope ($m$)**: $m = \tan \theta = \frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$.
    
- **Forms of the Equation of a Line**:
    
    - **Slope-Intercept**: $y = mx + c$.
        
    - **Point-Slope**: $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$.
        
    - **Two-Point**: $\frac{y-y_1}{x-x_1} = \frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$.
        
    - **Intercept Form**: $\frac{x}{a} + \frac{y}{b} = 1$.
        
- **Angle between two lines**: $\tan \theta = |\frac{m_1-m_2}{1+m_1m_2}|$.
    

#### **Special Conditions**

- **Parallel Lines**: $m_1 = m_2$.
    
- **Perpendicular Lines**: $m_1 \cdot m_2 = -1$.
    
- **Distance from Point $(x_1, y_1)$ to Line $ax+by+c=0$**: $d = \frac{|ax_1+by_1+c|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}$.
    
- **Pair of Straight Lines**: $ax^2 + 2hxy + by^2 = 0$ represents two lines passing through the origin.
    

> **Solved Example**: Find the equation of a line passing through $(2, 3)$ and perpendicular to $4x - 2y + 5 = 0$.
> 
> **Step 1**: Slope of given line ($m_1$): $y = 2x + 2.5 \Rightarrow m_1 = 2$.
> 
> **Step 2**: Perpendicular slope ($m_2$): $m_1 \cdot m_2 = -1 \Rightarrow 2 \cdot m_2 = -1 \Rightarrow m_2 = -1/2$.
> 
> **Step 3**: Use point-slope form: $y - 3 = -1/2(x - 2)$.
> 
> **Solution**: $2y - 6 = -x + 2 \Rightarrow x + 2y - 8 = 0$.

### 3. Circles

A circle is defined by its centre $(h, k)$ and radius $r$.

#### **Core Concepts & Formulas**

- **Standard Equation**: $(x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2$.
    
- **General Equation**: $x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$.
    
    - **Centre**: $(-g, -f)$.
        
    - **Radius**: $\sqrt{g^2+f^2-c}$.
        
- **Tangent at Point $(x_1, y_1)$**: $xx_1 + yy_1 + g(x+x_1) + f(y+y_1) + c = 0$.
    

#### **Special Cases**

- **Circle at Origin**: $x^2 + y^2 = r^2$.
    
- **Intercepts on Axes**: X-intercept $= 2\sqrt{g^2-c}$, Y-intercept $= 2\sqrt{f^2-c}$.
    

> **Solved Example**: Find the centre and radius of $x^2 + y^2 - 8x + 10y - 8 = 0$.
> 
> **Step 1**: $2g = -8 \Rightarrow g = -4$; $2f = 10 \Rightarrow f = 5$; $c = -8$.
> 
> **Step 2**: Centre $= (-g, -f) = (4, -5)$.
> 
> **Step 3**: Radius $= \sqrt{(-4)^2 + 5^2 - (-8)} = \sqrt{16 + 25 + 8} = \sqrt{49} = 7$.
> 
> **Solution**: Centre $(4, -5)$, Radius $7$.

### 4. Conic Sections: Parabola, Ellipse, Hyperbola

Conics are formed by the intersection of a plane and a double cone.

|**Feature**|**Parabola (y2=4ax)**|**Ellipse (a2x2​+b2y2​=1)**|**Hyperbola (a2x2​−b2y2​=1)**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Eccentricity ($e$)**|$e = 1$|$e = \sqrt{1 - \frac{b^2}{a^2}} < 1$|$e = \sqrt{1 + \frac{b^2}{a^2}} > 1$|
|**Focus**|$(a, 0)$|$(\pm ae, 0)$|$(\pm ae, 0)$|
|**Directrix**|$x = -a$|$x = \pm a/e$|$x = \pm a/e$|
|**Latus Rectum**|$4a$|$2b^2/a$|$2b^2/a$|

#### **Geometric Transformations**

The syllabus includes simple transformations:

- **Translation**: Shifting the origin to $(h, k)$ transforms $(x, y)$ to $(X+h, Y+k)$.
    
- **Rotation**: Rotating axes by angle $\theta$ transforms $(x, y)$ to $(x \cos \theta - y \sin \theta, x \sin \theta + y \cos \theta)$.
    

> **Solved Example**: Find the eccentricity of the ellipse $9x^2 + 25y^2 = 225$.
> 
> **Step 1**: Divide by 225: $\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$. Here $a^2=25, b^2=9$.
> 
> **Step 2**: $e = \sqrt{1 - b^2/a^2} = \sqrt{1 - 9/25} = \sqrt{16/25}$.
> 
> **Solution**: $e = 4/5 = 0.8$.
