
### 1. 2D Shapes: Areas, Triangles, and Quadrilaterals

Focuses on the surface region enclosed by two-dimensional figures.

#### **Core Concepts & Formulas**

- **Triangles**:
    
    - **General Area**: $\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$.
        
    - **Heron's Formula**: $\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$, where $s = \frac{a+b+c}{2}$.
        
    - **Equilateral Triangle**: $\text{Area} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^{2}$.
        
- **Quadrilaterals**:
    
    - **Rectangle**: $\text{Area} = \text{length} \times \text{breadth}$; $\text{Perimeter} = 2(l+b)$.
        
    - **Square**: $\text{Area} = \text{side}^{2}$; $\text{Perimeter} = 4a$.
        
    - **Parallelogram**: $\text{Area} = \text{base} \times \text{height}$.
        
    - **Rhombus**: $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times d_{1} \times d_{2}$.
        
    - **Trapezium**: $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times (\text{sum of parallel sides}) \times \text{height}$.
        

> **Solved Example**: Find the area of a triangle with sides 13 cm, 14 cm, and 15 cm.
> 
> **Step 1**: Calculate semi-perimeter: $s = (13+14+15)/2 = 21$ cm.
> 
> **Step 2**: Apply Heron's Formula: $\sqrt{21(21-13)(21-14)(21-15)}$.
> 
> **Step 3**: $\sqrt{21 \times 8 \times 7 \times 6} = \sqrt{7056}$.
> 
> **Solution**: Area = 84 sq. cm.

### 2. Circles

Covers the properties of circular regions and their boundaries.

#### **Core Concepts & Formulas**

- **Area**: $\pi r^{2}$.
    
- **Circumference**: $2\pi r$.
    
- **Circular Sector**:
    
    - **Area**: $\frac{1}{2}r^{2}\theta$ (where $\theta$ is in radians) or $\frac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^{2}$.
        
    - **Length of Arc**: $r\theta$ or $\frac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r$.
        

> **Solved Example**: Find the area of a circular sector with a radius of 7 cm and an angle of $60^{\circ}$.
> 
> **Step 1**: Formula $= \frac{60}{360} \times \pi \times (7)^{2}$.
> 
> **Step 2**: $\frac{1}{6} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 49 = \frac{1}{6} \times 154$.
> 
> **Solution**: Area $\approx$ 25.67 sq. cm.

### 3. 3D Solids: Volumes and Surface Areas

Focuses on the space occupied and the outer boundary of three-dimensional objects.

#### **Core Formulas for Simple Solids**

|**Solid**|**Volume (V)**|**Surface Area (S)**|
|---|---|---|
|**Cube**|$a^{3}$|$6a^{2}$ (Total)|
|**Cuboid**|$l \times b \times h$|$2(lb+bh+hl)$ (Total)|
|**Sphere**|$\frac{4}{3}\pi r^{3}$|$4\pi r^{2}$|
|**Cylinder**|$\pi r^{2}h$|$2\pi rh$ (Curved); $2\pi r(r+h)$ (Total)|
|**Cone**|$\frac{1}{3}\pi r^{2}h$|$\pi rl$ (Curved, where $l = \text{slant height}$)|

#### **Special Case: Prisms and Pyramids**

- **Prism**: $\text{Volume} = (\text{Area of base}) \times \text{height}$.
    
- **Pyramid**: $\text{Volume} = \frac{1}{3} \times (\text{Area of base}) \times \text{height}$.
    

> **Solved Example**: Find the volume and total surface area of a cylinder with radius 3 cm and height 7 cm.
> 
> **Step 1**: $\text{Volume} = \pi \times (3)^{2} \times 7 = \frac{22}{7} \times 9 \times 7$.
> 
> **Step 2**: $\text{Volume} = 198 \text{ cubic cm}$.
> 
> **Step 3**: $\text{TSA} = 2\pi r(r+h) = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 3 \times (3+7)$.
> 
> **Step 4**: $\text{TSA} = \frac{132}{7} \times 10 \approx 188.57 \text{ sq. cm}$.
> 
> **Solution**: Volume = 198 cm³, TSA $\approx$ 188.57 cm².
