## Part 6: Probability and Statistics

This section covers the mathematical framework for quantifying uncertainty and the methods for analyzing numerical data.

### 1. Basic Concepts of Probability Theory

Probability measures the likelihood of an event occurring, ranging from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain).

#### **Core Concepts & Formulas**

* **Classical Probability**: $P(E) = \frac{\text{Number of favorable outcomes}}{\text{Total number of possible outcomes}}$.
* **Complementary Events**: $P(E') = 1 - P(E)$.
* **Addition Rule**:
* For any two events: $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)$.
* For mutually exclusive events ($A \cap B = \emptyset$): $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B)$.


* **Multiplication Rule (Independent Events)**: $P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B)$.
* **Conditional Probability**: $P(A|B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)}$, where $P(B) > 0$.

> **Solved Example**: A bag contains 5 red and 3 blue balls. If two balls are drawn at random without replacement, what is the probability that both are red?
> **Step 1**: Total balls = 8. $P(\text{First is Red}) = 5/8$.
> **Step 2**: Remaining balls = 7 (4 red). $P(\text{Second is Red} | \text{First was Red}) = 4/7$.
> **Step 3**: $P(\text{Both Red}) = (5/8) \times (4/7) = 20/56$.
> **Solution**: $5/14$.

### 2. Measures of Central Tendency & Dispersion

These statistics summarize a data set by identifying its center and its spread.

#### **Core Concepts & Formulas**

* **Arithmetic Mean (Average)**: $\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x_i}{n}$.
* **Measures of Dispersion**:
* **Range**: $\text{Maximum value} - \text{Minimum value}$.
* **Variance ($\sigma^2$)**: $\frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{n}$.
* **Standard Deviation ($\sigma$)**: $\sqrt{\text{Variance}}$.


* **Skewness and Kurtosis**:
* **Skewness**: Measures the asymmetry of the distribution (Positive, Negative, or Zero).
* **Kurtosis**: Measures the "tailedness" or peakedness of the distribution.



> **Solved Example**: Find the standard deviation of the data: 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9.
> **Step 1**: Mean $(\bar{x}) = (2+4+4+4+5+5+7+9)/8 = 40/8 = 5$.
> **Step 2**: Squares of deviations: $(2-5)^2=9, (4-5)^2=1, (4-5)^2=1, (4-5)^2=1, (5-5)^2=0, (5-5)^2=0, (7-5)^2=4, (9-5)^2=16$.
> **Step 3**: Variance $= (9+1+1+1+0+0+4+16)/8 = 32/8 = 4$.
> **Solution**: Standard Deviation $= \sqrt{4} = 2$.

### 3. Random Variables and Distributions

Focuses on the mathematical functions that describe the probabilities of different outcomes.

#### **Core Concepts & Formulas**

* **Binomial Distribution**: $P(X=r) = {}^{n}C_r \cdot p^r \cdot q^{n-r}$ (where $p = \text{success probability}$, $q = 1-p$).
* **Mean** $= np$; **Variance** $= npq$.


* **Poisson Distribution**: $P(X=x) = \frac{e^{-\lambda} \lambda^x}{x!}$ (used for rare events).
* **Mean** = **Variance** $= \lambda$.


* **Normal Distribution**: A continuous, bell-shaped distribution defined by mean ($\mu$) and standard deviation ($\sigma$).

> **Solved Example**: A fair coin is tossed 6 times. Find the probability of getting exactly 2 heads.
> **Step 1**: $n=6, r=2, p=1/2, q=1/2$.
> **Step 2**: $P(X=2) = {}^{6}C_2 (1/2)^2 (1/2)^4 = 15 \times (1/2)^6 = 15/64$.
> **Solution**: $15/64$.

### 4. Correlation and Regression

Analyzes the relationship between two variables.

#### **Core Concepts & Formulas**

* **Correlation Coefficient ($r$)**: Measures the strength and direction of a linear relationship between $-1$ and $+1$.
* **Regression Line ($y$ on $x$)**: $y - \bar{y} = b_{yx}(x - \bar{x})$, where $b_{yx}$ is the regression coefficient.
* **Principle of Least Squares**: A method used to find the "best-fit" line by minimizing the sum of the squares of vertical deviations.

### 5. Special Case: Mathematical Expectations

The expected value $E(X)$ of a random variable is the long-run average value.

* **Formula**: $E(X) = \sum [x_i \cdot P(x_i)]$.

> **Solved Example**: A player tosses a die. He wins ₹10 if he gets a 6, and loses ₹2 for any other number. Find his mathematical expectation.
> **Step 1**: $P(6) = 1/6$; $P(\text{not } 6) = 5/6$.
> **Step 2**: $E(X) = (10 \times 1/6) + (-2 \times 5/6) = 10/6 - 10/6 = 0$.
> **Solution**: $0$ (The game is fair).
